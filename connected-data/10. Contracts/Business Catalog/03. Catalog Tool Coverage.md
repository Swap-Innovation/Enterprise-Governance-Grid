# Business Catalog — Catalog tool coverage

Research note mapping this pack’s asset types to leading catalog / glossary products.  
**Purpose:** keep Contracts aligned with Collibra Guided Stewardship + Business Glossary, and with Dataplex / OpenMetadata / Purview / Alation / Informatica equivalents.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Layer model (this pack)

| Layer | Role | Collibra analogue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glossary | Business vocabulary | Business Glossary (Term, Acronym, Measure/KPI, Category) |
| Conceptual | Context-independent data structure | Guided Stewardship conceptual (Data Domain, Data Concept) |
| Semantic (logical) | Bridge to physical | Guided Stewardship semantic (Data Model, Entity, Attribute) |
| Context & governance | Process, rule, policy, issue, report | Business Asset / Report Catalog / Stewardship |

Enterprise **meaning** SoR remains the [Semantic Control Plane](../Semantic%20Control%20Plane/) (DA-08 `mapsTo`). This pack is **prose / stewardship SoR** in Collibra (and peer glossaries).

## Coverage decisions in this pack

**In pack (16 types):** all rows above that are first-class business/stewardship objects.

**Explicitly out of this pack (Technical Catalog or elsewhere):**
- System, Database, Schema, Table, Column, File, Field (physical)
- Data Product, Port, Data Contract (Data Products pack)
- Namespace, Concept, Mapping Record, Federation Edge (Semantic Control Plane)
- Metric **calculation engines** (BI tools) — KPI may hold a descriptive Calculation Rule; authoritative calc stays beside the semantic layer ([SQ1](../../../docs/strategic-questions/SQ01-semantic-layer-definition.md))
